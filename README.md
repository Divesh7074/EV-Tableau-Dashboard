**Electric Vehicle (EV) Analytics Dashboard**

**Project Overview**

This project focuses on analyzing Electric Vehicle (EV) data to understand market trends, vehicle adoption, manufacturer performance, and geographical distribution.

An interactive Tableau dashboard is developed to transform raw EV data into meaningful insights that help understand the growth and development of the electric vehicle industry.

**Project Objectives**

Analyze the total number of electric vehicles.

Compare Battery Electric Vehicles (BEV) and Plug-in Hybrid Electric Vehicles (PHEV).

Identify top electric vehicle manufacturers.

Analyze EV adoption across different states.

Understand EV growth trends over the years.

Analyze vehicle distribution by model and electric range.

**Tools & Technologies**

Tableau – Dashboard Development and Data Visualization

Microsoft Excel – Data Cleaning and Data Preparation

Data Analytics – Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

**Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)**

Total Electric Vehicles

Average Electric Range

BEV Vehicles

PHEV Vehicles

EV Market Share

**Dashboard Analysis**

Vehicle Type Analysis: Comparison of BEV and PHEV vehicles.

Manufacturer Analysis: Identify leading EV manufacturers.

Geographical Analysis: Analyze EV distribution by state.

Year-wise Trend Analysis: Track electric vehicle adoption over time.

Model Analysis: Understand popular EV models.

Electric Range Analysis: Analyze the driving range of electric vehicles.

**Key Business Insights**

Understand electric vehicle adoption trends.

Identify leading manufacturers in the EV market.

Compare different types of electric vehicles.

Analyze geographical EV market distribution.

Support data-driven business decisions.
